Calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP) migraine treatment is a newer way to help prevent and treat migraine episodes.These medications block a protein called calcitonin gene-related peptide (CGRP). CGRP may cause inflammation and pain in the nervous system of people who have migraine attacks.CGRP migraine medications are also called anti-CGRPs, CGRP inhibitors, and CGRP antagonists. It also worsens headache pain and makes it last longer.CGRP levels can be elevated during a migraine episode and between episodes.
