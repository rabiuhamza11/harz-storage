Key takeaways Vertigo, a spinning sensation or imbalance, can sometimes indicate a stroke, particularly a brain stem stroke, which disrupts blood flow to the brain. While vertigo is a common symptom with various causes, recurring episodes should be evaluated by a healthcare professional, as they may signal a more serious underlying condition, including the potential for a stroke. Promptly seeking medical attention is crucial if stroke symptoms are suspected, as timely treatment can significantly improve recovery outcomes by restoring blood flow and minimizing brain damage.
