[Entertainment](https://dailypost.ng/entertainment/)‘Nigerians now have culture of begging’ – Omotola JaladePublished on October 17, 2026By [Sunny Green Itodo](https://dailypost.ng/author/sunny-green-itodo/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/17/nigerians-now-have-culture-of-begging-omotola-jalade/comments/)Veteran Nigerian actress, Omotola Jalade Ekeinde has criticised the entitlement mentality of the Nigerian society, lamenting that the country now has a culture of begging. Speaking in a recent episode of Activating Success With Love Idoko, the movie icon said she doesn’t support or encourage begging in any form except if it is a life-and-death situation. According to her, there is no reason anyone can’t work. She said, “A lot of Nigerians take opportunities for granted. And one thing we have to understand is that nobody owes you anything.
