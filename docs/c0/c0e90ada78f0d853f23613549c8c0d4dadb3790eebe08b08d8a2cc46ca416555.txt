He argued that rising poverty levels showed that the country was moving backwards despite its abundant human and natural resources.He cited figures indicating that Nigeria’s poverty rate rose from 40.1 per cent in 2019 to 56.2 per cent in 2023, with the rate projected to reach 63 per cent in 2026.According to him, millions of Nigerians are struggling to afford food and access healthcare despite
