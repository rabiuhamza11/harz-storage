Notice to the General Public on the Disposal of Physical Assets of Heritage Bank (IN-LIQ) N NDIC October 4, 2024 4 min read The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) wishes to draw the attention of the general public for the disposal of physical Assets belonging to Heritage Bank (In –Liquidation) at 47 affected branches. The date for the auctioning will be on Monday 14th October 2024 by 11am.The expression of interest will be for the Auction of chattels belonging to the defunct Heritage Bank across the country. Buyer who wish to participate shall follow laid down guide lines which is aimed at transparency, competitive, equitable and timeliness principles to recover the achievable financial returns for the Bank in –liquidation.All the Chattels in the location listed are to be auctioned therein. The property will be sold “as is “and “net “of all taxes.The Corporation shall give preference to Corporate bodies, financial institutions and individuals who are willing to buy all items at wholesale value before retail disposal of the physical assets.Auction exercise shall take place in all the Forty-seven (47) branches of the defunct Heritage Bank (in-liquidation) by competitively selected and Independent Auctioneers.The interested Public should contact the Corporation through the stated phone lines: 08029997846, 08036905830 for further inquiries.The affected branches by this phase of disposal are listed as follows:LIST OF HERITAGE BANK LOCATIONS FOR AUCTIONS/NLOCATIONSTATENO OF LOCATIONSDATES OF DISPOSAL1 FAULKS ROAD, ABA.ABIA1OCTOBER 14TH2PLOT K, KOPEK INDUSTRIAL ESTATE, MATORI, MUSHINLAGOS1OCTOBER 14TH 3 NO 74, ATIKU ABUBAKAR WAY, JIMETA, YOLA,ADAMAWA1OCTOBER 14TH 4 NO 41 AKA ROAD, UYO,AKWA IBOM1OCTOBER 14TH 5 NO 65 OZOMAGALA, ONITSHA,ANAMBRA5OCTOBER 14TH6 NO 3 MAIN MARKET, ONITSHA.ANAMBRAOCTOBER 15TH7NNAMDI AZIKWE UNIVERSITY AKWA,ANAMBRAOCTOBER 15TH8NO. 2, EDO EZEMEWI ROAD/BANK ROAD NNEWI,ANAMBRAOCTOBER 15TH93, BOURVITA ROAD, OGBARU MARKET, ONITSHA,ANAMBRAOCTOBER 15TH 10OGIRI-OKO ROAD, MAKURDIBENUE1OCTOBER 16TH 11 NO 343, NNEBISI ROAD, ASABA,DELTA1OCTOBER 16TH 12NO 17 SAPELE ROAD, HIGH COURT ROAD, BENINEDO2OCTOBER 16TH13 NO 130 AKPAKPAVA ROAD, BENIN,EDOOCTOBER 21TH 14NO 35, GANA STREET, MAITAMA, ABUJAFCT3OCTOBER 21TH 15OBAFEMI AWOLOWO WAY, UTAKO, ABUJAFCTOCTOBER 21TH16 COSCHARIS 1070 EGBE CLOSE/FASKARI STREET, GARKI, AREA 3 ABUJAFCTOCTOBER 21TH 17PLOT 47, NEW COMMERCIAL LAYOUT GOMBEGOMBE1OCTOBER 22TH 18AWOLOWO ROAD IKOYI,LAGOS OCTOBER 22TH 19LADIPO ROAD, MUSHIN,LAGOS OCTOBER 22TH20NO 6A AGUDOST STREET, ALABA INT.
