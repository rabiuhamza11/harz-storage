Hypercholesterolemia is also called high cholesterol. It refers to increased levels of cholesterol in the blood.Cholesterol is a type of lipid (fat), a waxy substance that helps build and maintain your cells, hormones, and some vitamins. People with high cholesterol levels are more prone to heart-related issues like heart attack and stroke.Unfortunately, hypercholesterolemia is a common issue among U.S. adults. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), almost 12% of people 20 years or older have high cholesterol.
