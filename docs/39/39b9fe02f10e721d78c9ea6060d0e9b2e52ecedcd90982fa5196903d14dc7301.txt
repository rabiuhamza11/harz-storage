[Entertainment](https://dailypost.ng/entertainment/)I’ve never been an advocate for marriage – Uzor ArukwePublished on October 16, 2026By [Roseline Nwosu](https://dailypost.ng/author/roseline-nwosu/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/16/ive-never-been-an-advocate-for-marriage-uzor-arukwe/comments/) Nollywood actor, Uzor Arukwe, has shared his views on marriage, stating that he has never been a strong advocate for the institution because he believes women bear more responsibilities and challenges in the union. The actor made this known during an interview on the In My Opinion podcast, where he discussed societal expectations surrounding marriage and the pressure often placed on women. According to Arukwe, women go through pregnancy and childbirth, take their husbands’ surnames and shoulder several other responsibilities associated with building a family.
