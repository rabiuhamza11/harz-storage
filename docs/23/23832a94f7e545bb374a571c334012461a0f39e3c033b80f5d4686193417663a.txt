Key takeaways Cognitive decline involves a gradual loss of thinking skills, including memory, reasoning, and attention. While some decline is typical with aging, significant changes could indicate a cognitive disorder. Doctors use various screening and neuropsychological tests, such as the SAGE test and MMSE, to detect cognitive decline, and experimental tests are being researched to identify changes even before symptoms appear. Adopting a healthy lifestyle, including a nutritious diet, regular physical activity, social engagement, and mentally stimulating activities, may help prevent or delay cognitive decline.
