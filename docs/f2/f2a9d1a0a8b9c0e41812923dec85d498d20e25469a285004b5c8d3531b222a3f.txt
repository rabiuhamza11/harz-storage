Working With Us Published: August 14, 2023 Thinking of working with us? Always committed to employee fulfillment and community development, the Securities and Exchange Commission is one of the best places to grow your career at any level. The Nigerian Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is the apex regulator of the Nigerian capital market. Our vision is “to be the leading capital market regulator in Africa,” and our mission is “to develop and regulate a capital market that is dynamic, fair, transparent and efficient to contribute to the nation’s economic development.” We are committed to protecting investors, maintaining market integrity, and promoting sustainable market growth.What It’s Like to Work at SECThe SEC is a vibrant, fast‐paced, and challenging environment—but also a rewarding one. Whether you are a fresh graduate, young professional or experienced hire, you’ll work alongside some of the brightest minds in the industry to make a real difference in the development of Nigeria’s economy and the futures of its investors. We value diversity and inclusion and strive to maintain a healthy work–life balance through competitive compensation, training programs, and supportive policies.Career OpportunitiesWe welcome applications from top talent across a wide range of disciplines. Our core recruiting focus includes:AccountancyLawEconomicsWe also periodically seek professionals in:Information TechnologyBusiness AdministrationThe HumanitiesWithin SEC you can specialize in areas such as compliance, corporate finance, enforcement, examinations, investor education, market regulation, policy and research, public relations, technology—and more.Our Recruitment ProcessTo ensure we only bring on board individuals who meet our high standards, all candidates undergo a structured Competency Assessment Centre. This rigorous process evaluates both the technical skills and behavioural competencies most relevant to the role. Only those who demonstrate the right blend of expertise, judgment, and commitment receive offers.Why Work at SEC?Make a difference: Contribute directly to a fair, transparent, and efficient capital market.Work with the best: Collaborate with experienced professionals and thought leaders.Competitive rewards: Enjoy a comprehensive salary and benefits package—including health insurance, retirement savings, and paid time off.Professional growth: Access ongoing training, mentorship, and clear pathways for advancement.Inclusive culture: Thrive in a workplace that values every voice and fosters mutual respect.How to ApplyVisit our [Openings Page](https://www.sec.gov.ng/about/careers/openings/) to see current vacancies and sign up for job alerts.We look forward to welcoming you to the SEC family!
