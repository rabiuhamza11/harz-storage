What if your home could work more naturally around your day? The air conditioner preparing the room while you are still on your way home. The refrigerator supporting food care in the kitchen. A dehumidifier helping manage indoor humidity. And when the day slows down, the television and audio system turning the living room into a place for football, films and music. This is the idea behind LG ThinQ: bringing compatible LG products together through one connected experience. Through the ThinQ app, users can manage supported LG products from one place, whether at home or away. Depending on the product and model, this can include remote control, monitoring energy use and accessing appliance information. For Nigerian consumers, the value is simple – less complexity, more convenience, and more time to enjoy life.
