Share Advertisement The Nigerian National Petroleum Company (NNPC) Limited has announced the activation of emergency containment protocols following a gas pipeline explosion in the Aleto community, Eleme Local Government Area of Rivers State. The blast, which occurred on Thursday on a pipeline operated by NNPC Gas Infrastructure Company Limited (NGIC), resulted in injuries to residents and damaged several commercial and residential properties in the area. To prevent further leakage or escalation, the company said it has shut off and isolated the affected pipeline segment. The state oil firm stated that it is collaborating with the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA), security agencies, and local emergency officials to coordinate relief and assess the environmental and structural damage. “We are deeply concerned by reports of injuries and damage to homes and businesses. Our thoughts are with those affected and their families. Protecting lives and preventing further harm are our immediate priorities. “NGIC has activated its emergency response procedures and isolated the affected pipeline segment to limit further impact and is working with the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority (NMDPRA), security agencies and other relevant stakeholders to coordinate the response and assess the impact on people, property and the environment. “We are also engaging the host community leadership to understand immediate needs and coordinate appropriate support for those affected,” the company stated in statement issued to journalists. It also explained that the cause of the incident and the full extent of its impact are currently being assessed, adding relevant operational information, including previous reports and actions taken, will be reviewed to support a clear understanding of the circumstances surrounding the incident. “We will continue to work closely with the relevant authorities to establish the facts and determine appropriate corrective measures. “Residents and members of the public are urged to keep clear of the affected area and follow safety instructions from emergency responders and the relevant authorities. NNPC Limited remains committed to the safety of its host communities, environmental protection and responsible operation of its facilities. We will provide further updates as verified information becomes available,” it added.
