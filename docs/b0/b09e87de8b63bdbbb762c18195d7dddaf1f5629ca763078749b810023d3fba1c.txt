[Entertainment](https://dailypost.ng/entertainment/)I panicked after Davido stopped talking to me – MayorkunPublished on October 17, 2026By [Sunny Green Itodo](https://dailypost.ng/author/sunny-green-itodo/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/17/i-panicked-after-davido-stopped-talking-to-me-mayorkun/comments/) Nigerian singer, Adewale Mayowa Emmanuel, popularly known as Mayorkun, has shared how Davido “miraculously” messaged and signed him after snubbing him for two weeks. Speaking in a recent episode of the Afropolitan podcast, Mayorkun explained that he had quit his banking job after Davido promised to sign him after seeing a clip of him singing online, but the DMW ghosted him for two weeks, which left him in panic. Mayorkun said he resorted to seeking spiritual assistance and started going to church, trusting God to intervene. He recalled that after leading a praise and worship session in the church one day, Davido texted him immediately and asked him to move into his house.
