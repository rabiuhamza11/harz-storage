STANDARDS ORGANISATION OF NIGERIA
What is SON?
SON was established by the SON Act No. 14, 2015, which repeals the Standards Organisation of Nigeria Act, Cap 59 laws of Federal Republic of Nigeria, 2004, and Enacts the Standards Organisation of Nigeria Act. 2015 for the purpose of providing additional functions for the organisation, increasing penalties for violations, and for related matters.

My experience with SON two years ago restored my faith and belief that Nigeria will be great again. I bought 7units of split units and shortly after installation, I discovered that two units of the products were not working well. I made several calls and complaints to the sale's outlet but nothing was done even though the products were still under warranty. I was frustrated to the point of given up when a friend advised me to contact S.O.N. I was reluctant to do so thinking it wasn't going to be futile. But on a second thought, I wrote to them and made my compliant. To my utmost surprise, I got a call from feedback and customer reconciliation department of S.O.N within 30mins. I was questioned by a female officer whose conduct I found very professional and impeccable. Two days after my discussion with S.O.N official, I got a call from the AC Brand informing me that they were coming to my house to check
the faulty product with
a view of replacing or fixing. I thought it was a joke but the next day, their technician came to my place to change the products with apologies. I was also asked to contact S.O.N and inform them that my products have been replaced which I did with gratitude to S.O.N. Since then, I have referred many persons who had similar cases to S.O.N and their stories are not different from mine. Great Organisation with passionate and professional staff. Thank you

The SON office asked the company to immediately address my complaints by fixing them.or totally replacing them. Several engineers were sent to my house to verify. This was something the company did not do initially. At the end of the day, the company was requested to change the ACs and/or return my money.
After further meetings and deliberations, the company finally obeyed the order of SON. Two new ACs were sent and installed in my house.
