Disposal of Landed Property, Microfinance Banks (In-Liquidation) N NDIC April 16, 2026 3 min read 1.The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC), in the exercise of its statutory mandate as the Liquidator of failed banks under section 62(1)(d) of NDIC Act, 2023 as amended, wishes to invite the general public for the upcoming disposal of Landed Properties belonging to some Micro Finance Banks (In-Liquidation) at 18 locations the Southern part of the country.Scope of Auction Exercise:2. The disposal process of the landed properties belonging to the Micro Finance Banks (In-Liquidation) shall be in a transparent and competitive bid. Bidders who wish to participate shall follow laid down guidelines which is aimed at ensuring, fair competition, accountability, equity and timeliness in line with tenets of corporate governance principles to enable value for money with commensurate financial returns from the auction exercise for the payment of liquidation dividends to uninsured depositors and creditors accordingly.3. The auction exercise shall be open and competitive to all bidders and shall take place at NDIC Lagos Office, Asset Management Department, (9th Floor), Mamman Kontagora House, 23A, Marina, Lagos.5. All interested bidders should submit their sealed bids with 10% bid security in Bank Draft in favour of the closed Micro Finance Bank and drop at the NDIC Lagos Office, Asset Management Department, (9th Floor), Mamman Kontagora House, 23A, Marina, Lagos on or before 30th April, 2026. Failure to attach the 10% Bank Draft for bid security will render such bids submitted Null and Void.6. Successful bidder would be give two weeks from the date of the bid opening to pay the balance of 90% of the bided amount. Please note that any successful bid that failed to make final payment within the stipulated time frame shall forfeit their 10% amount as penalty charges.7. where they are sitting tenant or the property is on a long lease, the tenant OR lease holder would be given the right of first refusal- but subject to competitive bid.8.
