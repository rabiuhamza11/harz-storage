Key takeaways Bipolar I disorder is characterized by manic episodes, while Bipolar II disorder involves hypomanic episodes and a major depressive episode. Although Bipolar I is often considered more severe due to the intensity of manic episodes, depressive episodes can be more frequent and prolonged in Bipolar II disorder. Both Bipolar I and Bipolar II disorders can be managed effectively with a combination of medication and psychotherapy, along with lifestyle adjustments and strong support systems.
