NDIC and Federal High Court Strengthen Partnership for Efficient Justice and Financial System Protection The NDIC Management, led by Managing Director/Chief Executive Mr. Thompson Oludare Sunday, hosted the Chief Registrar of the Federal High Court, Mr. Yahaya Yakubu Shafa, and his team on a strategic courtesy visit aimed at deepening institutional collaboration. N NDIC June 22, 2026 1 min read The NDIC Management, led by Managing Director/Chief Executive Mr. Thompson Oludare Sunday, hosted the Chief Registrar of the Federal High Court, Mr. Yahaya Yakubu Shafa, and his team on a strategic courtesy visit aimed at deepening institutional collaboration.The discussions centered on accelerating the objective resolution of NDIC-related cases, with particular focus on failed banks, debt recovery, and liquidation proceedings. Both institutions recognized the critical importance of judicial efficiency in protecting depositor interests and ensuring swift asset recovery.The partnership reflects the complementary roles of both institutions in maintaining financial system stability. While the Federal High Court provides judicial oversight and resolution mechanisms, the NDIC safeguards depositor funds and manages the resolution process for failed financial institutions.Both institutions pledged their commitment to enhancing judicial efficiency to ensure depositors are protected and assets of banks in-liquidation are recovered promptly. This collaborative approach acknowledges that effective justice delivery directly impacts the confidence stakeholders maintain in the financial system.The visit underscored the importance of inter-institutional coordination in addressing the complex legal and financial challenges associated with bank failures. By streamlining case resolution processes and improving communication channels, both organizations aim to reduce the time required to resolve disputes and recover assets.This strengthened partnership demonstrates the NDIC's proactive engagement in creating an enabling environment for efficient financial system management, ultimately benefiting depositors and contributing to overall financial system resilience in Nigeria.
