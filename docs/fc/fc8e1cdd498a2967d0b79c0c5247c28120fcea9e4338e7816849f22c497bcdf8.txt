NDIC Hosts International Deposit Insurers' Africa Regional Meeting in Abuja The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) hosted the 2026 International Association of Deposit Insurers (IADI) Africa Regional Committee (ARC) Annual Meeting and Workshop in Abuja, bringing together prominent deposit insurance leaders from across the continent. N NDIC July 20, 2026 1 min read The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) hosted the 2026 International Association of Deposit Insurers (IADI) Africa Regional Committee (ARC) Annual Meeting and Workshop in Abuja, bringing together prominent deposit insurance leaders from across the continent.The high-profile gathering commenced with sessions of the Research and Publicity Technical Committees, followed by the main ARC Annual Meeting presided over by Julia Oyet, Chairperson of IADI-ARC and CEO of the Deposit Protection Fund of Uganda.The event attracted distinguished participants, including IADI Secretary-General Eva Hupkes and Chief Executive Officers of deposit insurance agencies representing countries across Africa. This international assembly provided a crucial platform for sharing expertise, best practices, and innovative approaches to deposit insurance and financial system protection.The meeting facilitated discussions on emerging challenges in the deposit insurance sector, including technological advancements, regulatory frameworks, and depositor protection mechanisms. The participation of continental leaders underscored Africa's commitment to strengthening financial system resilience and stability.Hosting this significant international event demonstrated Nigeria's leadership position in the African financial services landscape and the NDIC's recognition as a key institutional player in deposit insurance matters. The workshop sessions enabled delegates to exchange insights on research initiatives, publicity strategies, and industry developments affecting deposit insurers across the continent.The successful hosting of the IADI-ARC meeting reinforced the NDIC's role as a responsible custodian of depositor funds and a committed partner in promoting financial system stability throughout Africa.
