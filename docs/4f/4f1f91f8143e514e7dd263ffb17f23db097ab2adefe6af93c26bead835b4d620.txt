Key Takeaways Sadness is a temporary emotion triggered by upsetting events, whereas depression is a persistent mental health condition that affects various aspects of life. See a doctor if your sadness lasts more than 2 weeks. Risk factors like traumatic events, family history, and medical conditions can increase the likelihood of depression, but a diagnosis requires assessment by a healthcare professional using the DSM-5 criteria. Both sadness and depression can be managed through lifestyle changes. However, depression often requires additional interventions, such as therapy or medication, to help with recovery and healing.
