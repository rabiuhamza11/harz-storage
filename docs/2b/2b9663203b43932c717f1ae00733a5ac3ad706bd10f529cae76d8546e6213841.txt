NOTICE TO GENERAL PUBLIC FOR DISPOSAL OF LANDED PROPERTY AND OTHER PHYSICAL ASSETS OF HERITAGE BANK (IN-LIQUIDATION) N NDIC November 11, 2024 5 min read The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC), in the exercise of its statutory powers as liquidator of failed banks under section 62(1)(d) of NDIC Act, 2023, wishes to draw the attention of the general public for the upcoming disposal of landed property and physical Assets belonging to Heritage Bank (In-Liquidation) at 36 affected locations across the country. The date for the planned auction exercise will commence from Wednesday 4th December 2024 by 10:00am Prompt.Scope of Disposal:1. The scope of disposal will be for the Auction of landed property and chattels belonging to the Heritage Bank (In-Liquidation) across the country. Buyers who wish to participate shall follow laid down guidelines aimed at ensuring transparency, fair competition, accountability, equity and timeliness in line with the tenets of corporate governance principles to enable recovery of commensurate values from the auction exercise for the payment of liquidation dividends to eligible claimants.2. Landed property and Chattels in the affected location as listed are to be auctioned therein. The property will be sold “as is “and “net “of all taxes.3. The Corporation shall give preference to financial institutions who are willing to buy the property at the highest auctioned prices along with all the physical assets at wholesale value, thereby giving them the right to match the highest bid. The purpose of this preference is to allow the continuation of provision of financial services to the Nigerian public at those locations to bolster financial inclusion initiatives of the Nigerian financial services regulators.4. However, Corporate bodies and Private individuals willing to compete are equally eligible to compete with the financial institutions without prejudice.5. The auction exercise shall be open and competitive to all bidders and shall take place at the six (6) geo-political zones covering all the thirty-six (36) locations of the Heritage Bank (In-Liquidation) at scheduled dates accordingly.6. All interested bidders should, in addition, submit their sealed bids with 10% bid security in Bank draft at various designated NDIC locations across the country. Failure to attach the 10% Bank draft for bid security will render such bids submitted Null and Void.7.
