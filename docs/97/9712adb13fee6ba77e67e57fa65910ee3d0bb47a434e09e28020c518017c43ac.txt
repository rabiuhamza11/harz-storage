Building on previous editions, the three-day event is expected to attract participants from different countries, with organisers anticipating testimonies of healing and personal transformation. The final edition of 2026 is expected to draw participants seeking to experience what they describe as the love, mercy and healing power of God. Registrants are encouraged to indicate whether they or their loved ones need healing so they can receive guidance and access preparatory faith classes and materials. The invitation extends beyond those seeking physical healing. Pastor Chris Oyakhilome, convener of the event, has repeatedly emphasised the importance of God’s Word, describing the programme as an opportunity for people of all ages and circumstances to encounter the message of Christ, strengthen their faith and experience God’s presence. One message, every language, every nation As in previous editions, the event will be streamed live and translated into multiple languages and major dialects, bringing together participants from diverse cultural backgrounds. According to the organisers, millions of Healing Centres have been established in towns, villages, hospitals, prisons, orphanages, homes and other locations worldwide to enable individuals, families and groups to participate. ‘We can change their world’ — Pastor Chris calls for global participation Ahead of the event, Pastor Chris Oyakhilome has called for a global mobilisation to ensure that more people can participate in the programme. Speaking recently during the Global Communion Service, he said: “It’s just a few more days; we will have the Healing Streams Live Healing Services, and it is going to be huge. By the power of the Holy Spirit, the healing graces of God, the Angels of God, will cover this world, and many will be healed; especially in this month of mercies.” He highlighted the programme’s importance to people seeking relief from illnesses and urged ministers of the Gospel worldwide to work together to expand its reach. “We have to mobilize all that the Lord has given us authority to control and to influence. Get everyone ready for Healing Streams. We can change their world.” Referring to testimonies from previous editions, Pastor Chris said the programme could bring blessings to people of all ages. “This is something that will bring so much blessing to old and young alike. Think about babies, children who have never walked because of some debilitating disease. And during the Healing Streams, they will begin to walk. The deaf will hear, the dumb will speak, the blind will see; God’s going to change their world completely because of the Healing Streams. Even the dead will be raised,” he said. He urged people to prepare for the event, expressing his belief that it would provide an opportunity for people worldwide to experience God’s glory. “I want you to look forward to Healing Streams, prepare for it, plan for it; because the glory of God will be made manifest around the world,” he added. A legacy of testimonies According to the organisers, previous editions of the programme have generated numerous testimonies of reported healings and personal transformations, including accounts involving people who had experienced serious health conditions. The organisers say these testimonies, shared by participants from different countries and backgrounds, have continued to generate interest in subsequent editions of the Healing Streams Live Healing Services. Expectations rise across nations With the October edition approaching, participants from different countries have expressed their expectations for the programme. Sakshi Telure from India said she was looking forward to the event and encouraged others to share information about it with their friends and families. “I’m filled with expectation for this edition of the Healing Streams Live Healing Services. I believe even more amazing things are going to happen this time, and I’m excited to share the news with everyone I know. “I encourage you to share it with your friends and family because I believe that, whether you need healing, restoration, or a breakthrough in any area of your life, you can receive your miracle. I’m so excited and full of expectation for what God is going to do. I believe many people will experience the Lord’s grace, mercy, and love through the Healing Streams Live Healing Services. Praise the Lord!” she said. Shabib from Nigeria said he was hoping for improvements in his health and finances. “I’m expecting a miracle in my health and finances in this edition. I believe the pain I have suffered for many years due to a spinal injury will finally leave me permanently, along with the high blood pressure and weakness that have plagued me. My faith is high, and my expectation shall not be cut off,” he said. Vanessa from Canada said she hoped the event would reach people who had lost hope, particularly in her country. “I’m expecting this Healing Streams to reach people who may have lost hope and their way, especially in my country. I believe many will encounter the healing power of God and experience miracles they never thought possible, and, most importantly, receive salvation,” she said. Steve from Germany also expressed optimism about the forthcoming edition. “I expect that this edition will surpass everything we have witnessed before. I’m looking forward to countless testimonies of healing, restoration, and salvation from people across the nations,” he said. Global mobilisation intensifies Organisers say preparations for the event are intensifying, with individuals and groups mobilising participants in communities around the world. Activities include establishing Healing Centres, distributing Healing to the Nations magazines and participating in a 24-hour prayer chain, through which Christians are praying for the sick and those expected to participate in the programme. The mobilisation is aimed at expanding awareness of the event and encouraging more people to take part in the final edition of the year. Road to Healing Streams returns with season 18 As part of the preparations, Road to Healing Streams Live Healing Services returns with its 18th season, featuring reports on preparations, testimonies and faith-based activities taking place ahead of the event. The programme airs daily on Healing Streams TV at 12 noon and 4 pm (GMT+1) until Thursday, October 22. It features activities by ministries, churches, organisations, volunteers and individuals preparing their communities through healing crusades, evangelism campaigns, prayer initiatives, Healing Centre mobilisation and community outreach programmes. The broadcast also features testimonies from participants who attribute reported improvements in their conditions to their involvement in previous editions of the programme. Reported healing testimonies from the July edition José Marín — Panama According to the organisers, José Marín had experienced severe pain and limited mobility in his leg and spine for two years following an accident. He reportedly experienced relief during the July edition of the Healing Streams Live Healing Services after Pastor Chris prayed for participants seeking healing. The organisers said Marín subsequently reported that the pain had disappeared and his mobility had improved. Cheany’s son — Vietnam The organisers said Cheany’s son had spent 28 days on a ventilator after his right lung collapsed. According to the account, Cheany participated in the programme from her son’s hospital bedside and laid hands on him while Pastor Chris prayed. She subsequently reported an improvement in her son’s condition. Vanessa — Canada Vanessa reportedly lived with type 2 diabetes for five years and experienced neuropathy, vision problems, pain and swelling in her feet. Following her participation in the July edition, she reported that the burning sensation, tingling and swelling had disappeared and that she could walk, sit and stand more comfortably. Pauline Atu — Nigeria Pauline Atu from Cross River State reportedly suffered from arthritis for a year, making walking, running, jumping, sitting and sleeping difficult because of pain. According to the organisers, she experienced an improvement during the Healing Streams programme and subsequently reported being able to walk, run and jump freely. Adiel — Ecuador Five-year-old Adiel reportedly had difficulty speaking from birth. According to the organisers, his mother brought him to a Healing Streams centre in the hope that he would receive healing. During the programme, he reportedly began speaking more clearly and subsequently communicated more fluently. The accounts above are testimonies reported by participants or organisers and have not been independently medically verified. How to participate The Healing Streams Live Healing Services will hold from October 23 to 25, 2026, with daily sessions beginning at 2 pm (GMT+1). The organisers said the services would be streamed on Healing Streams TV, LoveWorld Networks and selected terrestrial television and radio stations worldwide. Registration is free through the programme’s website.
