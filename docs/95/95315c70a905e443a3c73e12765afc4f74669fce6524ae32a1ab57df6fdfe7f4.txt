[Entertainment](https://dailypost.ng/entertainment/)Tope Alabi, Yinka Alaseyori, Bidemi Olaoba, Ebuka Songs mourn gospel singer, ToluwanisingsPublished on October 16, 2026By [Racheal Ayodele](https://dailypost.ng/author/racheal-ayodele/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/16/tope-alabi-yinka-alaseyori-bidemi-olaoba-ebuka-songs-mourn-gospel-singer-toluwanisings/comments/) Nigerian gospel singers, Tope Alabi, Yinka Alaseyori, Bidemi Olaoba, Peterson and Ebuka Songs have mourned the death of their colleague, Toluwanisings. DAILY reports that Toluwanisings slumped at his Ibadan residence on Wednesday while about taking his children to school. According to his PA, Babadunni Gbolabo, the singer died after being rejected by seven hospitals in Ibadan, Oyo State. While tagging the incident as a big shock, Tope Alabi wrote, “This is such a big shock! I still can’t wrap my head around this. It hurts, but I will not sorrow as those who have no hope.
