SEC and the Nigeria Sanctions Committee Published: November 15, 2023 The SEC is a member of the Nigeria Sanctions Committee, a body responsible for implementing targeted financial sanctions in support of national and international security objectives. The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) plays a crucial role in the implementation of targeted financial sanctions, collaborating closely with the Nigeria Sanctions Committee (NigSac) to safeguard the integrity of the Nigerian capital market and combat the financing of terrorism and other illicit activities. This partnership ensures that the Nigerian capital market adheres to international standards and contributes to global efforts to counter financial crime.The Nigeria Sanctions Committee: Enforcing Targeted Financial SanctionsEstablished in 2022, NigSac is the national body responsible for implementing targeted financial sanctions under the United Nations Security Council Resolutions (UNSCRs). NigSac's mandate includes:Identifying and designating individuals and entities subject to targeted financial sanctionsFreezing the assets of designated persons and entitiesProhibiting transactions with designated persons and entitiesImplementing travel bans on designated personsThe SEC's Role in Sanctions EnforcementThe SEC plays a critical role in NigSac's work by:Identifying and verifying the identities of designated persons and entitiesFreezing the assets of designated persons and entities held within the capital marketDisseminating sanctions information to regulated entitiesInvestigating potential violations of sanctions regulationsStrengthening Cooperation for Effective Sanctions ImplementationThe SEC and NigSac have established a strong working relationship, ensuring seamless coordination and information sharing. This collaboration has been instrumental in:Timely identification and designation of individuals and entities subject to sanctionsEffective freezing of assets and prevention of prohibited transactionsEnhanced compliance with sanctions regulations by market participantsLearn More About Sanctions ComplianceTo learn more about NigSac's activities and the SEC's role in sanctions enforcement, we encourage you to visit the NigSac website [www.nigsac.gov.ng](https://nigsac.gov.ng/) Through its active participation in the NigSac's work, the SEC remains committed to safeguarding the integrity of the Nigerian capital market, protecting investors, and upholding the nation's international obligations in combating financial crime.
