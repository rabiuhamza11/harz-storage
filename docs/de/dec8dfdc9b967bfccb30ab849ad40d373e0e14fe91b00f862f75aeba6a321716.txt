Notice to Beneficiaries, Allottees and General Public, Housing Estates, Ahocol Savings and Loans Ltd. (In-Liquidation) N NDIC August 26, 2025 1 min read The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC), official liquidator of the defunct AHOCOL SAVINGS AND LOANS LTD. (In-Liquidation), hereby calls on allottees and other interested parties of the following underlisted estates previously acquired by the failed bank and now under the custody of the NDIC as Liquidator, that a verification exercise and physical reconfirmation of Land Allocation will be carried out by NDIC officials on the scheduled dates below. The estates are:1)Legacy Housing Estate (Phase 1, 2 & 3), Mgbakwu – 27th Wednesday, 2025, 2pm – 5pm2)Chukwuemaka Housing Estate (Phase 1 & 2), Mgbakwu – Thursday 28th August, 2025, 9am-5pm3) Ifeodioranma International Housing Estate (Phase 1 & 2), Mbakwu– Friday 29th, 2025, 9am – 5pm4)University Campus Estate (Phase 1 & 2), Ukwulu – 9am – 5pm, Friday 29th, 20255)Millennium Housing Estate (Phase 1 & 2), Urum – 9am – 5pm, Saturday, August 30th, 20256)Oriental Housing Estate, Aguilera – 9am-5pm, Saturday, August 30th, 2025VENUE: Emperor Melaine Hotel, Unizik Junction, along Enugu/Onitsha express way, besides Parktonia hotel, Awka, AnambraPlease Note that adherence to the above stated times and dates is mandatory.
