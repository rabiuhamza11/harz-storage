Key takeaways Early diagnosis and treatment of wet macular degeneration are crucial in preventing significant vision loss. Current treatments involve regular injections of anti-VEGF medication to slow down the growth of abnormal blood vessels in the eye. Ongoing research is focused on developing new, more convenient treatments like longer-lasting injections, gene therapy, eye drops, and oral medications to reduce the need for frequent eye injections.
