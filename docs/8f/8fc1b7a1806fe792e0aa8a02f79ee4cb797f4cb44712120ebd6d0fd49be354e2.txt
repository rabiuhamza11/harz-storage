NDIC Hosts Deposit Protection Fund Uganda on Strategic Study Visit Program The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) hosted the Board and Management of the Deposit Protection Fund (DPF) of Uganda on a comprehensive five-day study visit program. N NDIC April 2, 2026 1 min read The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) hosted the Board and Management of the Deposit Protection Fund (DPF) of Uganda on a comprehensive five-day study visit program. The visit provided Ugandan counterparts with key insights into the critical activities and achievements of the NDIC as a strategic regional leader in deposit insurance.The study program was designed to showcase the NDIC's operational excellence in depositor protection and demonstrate best practices that have established the Corporation as a recognized leader within the African deposit insurance community. The five-day format allowed sufficient time for detailed discussions, facility tours, and comprehensive knowledge transfer across various functional areas.Through this engagement, the DPF Uganda gained practical understanding of the NDIC's strategies for maintaining high coverage of depositor accounts, managing bank resolution processes efficiently, and implementing risk monitoring frameworks. The visit also provided opportunities to discuss common challenges faced by African deposit insurers and explore collaborative solutions.Such bilateral visits strengthen regional cooperation within the African deposit insurance community and facilitate the exchange of best practices among institutions with similar mandates. By hosting international delegations, the NDIC positions itself as a knowledge center and institutional leader capable of sharing experiences with peer organizations.The strategic visit demonstrates the NDIC's commitment to regional cooperation and institutional capacity building across Africa's financial sector. These engagements contribute to strengthening deposit insurance systems continent-wide, ultimately benefiting millions of depositors across the region.The successful hosting of the DPF Uganda delegation reinforces the NDIC's reputation as a credible, professional institution dedicated to advancing deposit insurance excellence throughout Africa.
