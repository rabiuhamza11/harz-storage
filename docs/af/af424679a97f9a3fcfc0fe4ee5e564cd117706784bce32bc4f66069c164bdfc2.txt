Key takeaways Ulcerative colitis and some of the medications used to treat it may cause skin symptoms for some, including rashes, psoriasis, and acne. Conditions linked to UC include erythema nodosum, pyoderma gangrenosum, and Sweet syndrome, each with distinct symptoms and treatments. If you have UC and notice skin changes, consult a healthcare professional for evaluation and management, which might involve oral or topical medications. Ulcerative colitis (UC) is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) that affects the large intestine, but it can also cause skin issues. These can include painful rashes.Some rashes are the result of inflammation within your body. Other skin issues linked to UC may be caused by medication used to treat the condition.
