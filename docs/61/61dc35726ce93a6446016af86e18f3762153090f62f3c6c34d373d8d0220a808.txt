FEDERAL MINISTRY OF INDUSTRY, TRADE AND INVESTMENT HOLDS TOP MANAGEMENT MEETING The Director General/Chief Executive, Standards Organisation of Nigeria. Dr. Ifeanyi Chukwunonso Okeke, and other Chief Executive Officers (CEOs) under the Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment participated in a top management meeting organised by the Honourable Minister, Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment. Jumoke Oduwole and the Minister for Industry, Senator John Owah Enoh on Tuesday in Abuja. The meeting was set to review the Ministry’s performance for the 1st Quarter of the year 2025 in preparation for the submission to the Central Results Delivery Coordinator Unit (CRDCU). The meeting brought together the Ministry’s Permanent Secretary, Ambassador Nuru Abba, CEOs of the 20 Agencies under the Ministry and Directors of the Ministry for strategic discussions. The highlight of the meeting was the presentation of a report on the 2024 FMITI deliverables performance; a report on the revised performance bond; a presentation of January and February Central Delivery Coordination Unit’s monthly reports and the presentation of reports on the implementation of three (3) game changing projects.
