[Metro](https://dailypost.ng/metro/)Oyo: Police arrest three men over alleged homosexual actsPublished on October 17, 2026By [Sunday Dennis](https://dailypost.ng/author/sunday-dennis/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/17/oyo-police-arrest-three-men-over-alleged-homosexual-acts/comments/) Police operatives in Oyo State have arrested three men in Saki over allegations of engaging in same-sex acts. The suspects were identified as Ayomide Lukeman, 22, Shuaib Aremu, 22, and Abdul-Rasheed Mufeed, 23. They were apprehended by operatives of the Oyo State Police Command Monitoring Unit, Saki Operational Base. The command’s spokesperson, DSP Olayinka Ayanlade, said the arrests followed intelligence received on September 29, 2026, regarding the alleged involvement of some individuals in what the police described as unnatural sexual conduct. He said two of the men allegedly confessed to having had sexual relations with the third suspect on several occasions, adding that they reportedly referred to the latter as their “wife”.
