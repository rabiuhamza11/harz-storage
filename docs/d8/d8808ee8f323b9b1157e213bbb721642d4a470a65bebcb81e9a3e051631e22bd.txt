Key takeaways Rexulti is a prescription medication used to treat certain mental health conditions, such as depression. This drug can cause side effects, including weight gain, sleepiness, and feeling restless. More serious side effects are possible with Rexulti, but these are not common. Rexulti can interact with some medications, such as certain antibiotics.
