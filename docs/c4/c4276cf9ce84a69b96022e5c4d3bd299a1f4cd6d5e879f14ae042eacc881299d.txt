Key takeaways Antidepressants balance brain chemicals called neurotransmitters to ease depression symptoms, with several classes available, including SSRIs, SNRIs, and MAOIs. Side effects vary among antidepressants, but may include nausea, insomnia, or more serious risks like serotonin syndrome and suicidal thoughts, necessitating close monitoring by a healthcare professional. Finding the right antidepressant may involve trial and error, considering factors like overall health, potential side effects, medication costs, and the frequency of doses.
