In comments to reporters on Wednesday, Trump said the idea could become a "hostile act." He threatened to impose sanctions on the EU in response. "If they do that, if I think it's at all a hostile act, I will put very serious tariffs or stop trading with Europe on many things," Trump told reporters en route to an event in North Carolina. The US president also described the prospect of Canada joining the EU as "laughable." Why is Canada turning to the EU? The potential Canadian EU associate membership comes as Trump, during his second term in office, has shaken Washington's relationship with its northern neighbor to the core. Canada was among the first victims of Trump's tariff policy.
