SEC and the West African Securities Regulators Association (WASRA) Published: November 15, 2023 The SEC collaborates with the West African Securities Regulators Association (WASRA), a regional body that facilitates cooperation and information exchange among West African securities regulators. The Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) is a founding member and active participant in the West African Securities Regulators Association (WASRA), a regional body dedicated to promoting the harmonization of securities regulation and enhancing investor protection across West Africa. This partnership has been instrumental in fostering a more integrated and efficient capital market ecosystem within the region.WASRA: Advancing Regulatory Harmonization in West AfricaFounded in 2015, WASRA brings together securities regulators from 15 West African countries, with the mission to:Harmonize securities regulation and promote a level playing field in the regionStrengthen investor protection and market integrityEnhance market transparency and efficiencyFacilitate cross-border capital flows and investmentsFoster capacity building and knowledge sharing among regulatorsThe SEC's Pioneering Role in WASRAThe SEC has been a driving force behind WASRA's establishment and success, playing a key role in shaping the organization's vision and strategic direction. The SEC's leadership contributions include:Hosting the inaugural meeting of WASRA in 2015Providing technical expertise and guidance in developing WASRA's regulatory frameworkChampioning initiatives to promote cross-border listings and tradingAdvocating for enhanced investor education and protectionKey Achievements of the SEC-WASRA PartnershipThe SEC-WASRA partnership has yielded significant milestones in promoting regional capital market integration, including:Adoption of the WASRA Common Core Principles for Securities RegulationEstablishment of a regional cross-border surveillance systemDevelopment of harmonized regulatory requirements for collective investment schemesImplementation of investor education initiatives across the regionExplore WASRA's InitiativesTo learn more about WASRA's initiatives and its impact on the development of West African capital markets, we encourage you to visit the WASRA website [www.wasra-amfao.org](https://www.wasra-amfao.org/) Through its active engagement with WASRA, the SEC remains committed to promoting a harmonized and well-regulated capital market in West Africa, fostering regional economic growth and prosperity.
