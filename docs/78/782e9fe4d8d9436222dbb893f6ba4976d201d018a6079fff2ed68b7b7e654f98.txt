There are four types of chronic asthma, categorized based on how often and how severe your symptoms are. This can range from mild or intermittent to severe or persistent. Acute asthma, often called an asthma attack, involves a sudden worsening of symptoms and can be life threatening. It requires immediate treatment and possibly hospitalization. Asthma is a chronic medical condition that causes breathing difficulties. These difficulties result from the airways narrowing and swelling.
