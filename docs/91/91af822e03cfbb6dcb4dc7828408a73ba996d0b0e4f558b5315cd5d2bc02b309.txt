Key Takeaways Basal metabolic rate (BMR) is the minimum number of calories your body burns at rest for basic functions like breathing, circulation, and cell production. You can estimate it using the Harris-Benedict formula, which factors in your weight, height, age, and sex. Knowing your BMR can help you determine how many calories to consume daily to gain, lose, or maintain your weight. However, many factors influence weight beyond calorie counting, including physical activity level, metabolism, and what you eat. You can change your BMR by increasing muscle mass through resistance training. Multiplying your BMR by an activity factor (1.2 for sedentary to 1.9 for extra active) gives you an approximate daily calorie need.
