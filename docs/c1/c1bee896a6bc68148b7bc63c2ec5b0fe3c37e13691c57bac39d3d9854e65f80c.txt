Key takeaways Psoriatic arthritis (PsA) combines joint pain and swelling with psoriasis, affecting up to 30% of people with psoriasis. Symptoms vary and can range from mild to severe, affecting mobility and daily activities. Early diagnosis and treatment are recommended. Treatment for PsA aims to improve symptoms like skin rash and joint inflammation.
