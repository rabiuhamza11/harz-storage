Expenditure EconomicsExpenditure Department prepares the annual budget of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and also releases monthly and quarterly warrants and AIE. i. Organisation of annual Pre-Budget Workshops for Ministries/Extra-Ministerial Departments; ii. Issuing of Annual Call Circulars to all Federal Ministries, Extra-Ministerial Departments, State-owned Companies and Corporations; iii. Preparation of Recurrent and Capital Budget for all Federal Ministries, Extra-Ministerial Departments, State-owned Companies and Corporations; iv. Publication of Annual Budget Book; v. Control of Expenditure in respect of Reserved Votes; vi. Assessment and analysis of requests for extra-budgetary Capital and Recurrent Expenditure from Federal Ministries, Extra-Ministerial Departments, State-owned Companies and Corporations; vii. Release of Funds through Recurrent and Capital Development Fund General Warrants Quarterly and by means of Authority to Incur Expenditure (AIE); viii. Examination of Returns and Progress Reports on expenditure from Federal Ministries, Extra-Ministerial Departments, State-owned Companies and Corporations; ix Preparation of annual report on Federal government expenditure.
