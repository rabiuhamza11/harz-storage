Key takeaways Sleep has two phases: non-rapid eye movement (NREM) and rapid eye movement (REM). It also has four stages that repeat in 4 to 6 cycles nightly. Each stage affects your body differently, from light sleep to deep, restorative sleep that supports tissue repair, immune strength, and memory. Sleep disorders like insomnia, obstructive sleep apnea (OSA), restless legs syndrome (RLS), and narcolepsy can reduce sleep quality, but sleep hygiene and treatments may help. Sleep is one of the most important activities for your health.
