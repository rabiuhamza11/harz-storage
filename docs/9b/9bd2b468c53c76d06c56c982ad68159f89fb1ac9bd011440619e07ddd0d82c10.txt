NDIC Reinforces Financial Safety Net Role Amid Banking Sector Reforms The Managing Director/Chief Executive of the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC), Mr. Thompson O. Sunday, delivered a goodwill address at the House Committee on Insurance & Actuarial Matters' Retreat in Lagos N NDIC August 14, 2026 1 min read The Managing Director/Chief Executive of the Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC), Mr. Thompson O. Sunday, delivered a goodwill address at the House Committee on Insurance & Actuarial Matters' Retreat in Lagos, focusing on "Strengthening the Financial Safety Net amid Banking Recapitalisation & Fintech Innovation."During the engagement, Mr. Sunday underscored the NDIC's critical position as a core component of Nigeria's financial safety net. The Corporation protects depositors and promotes financial system stability through its comprehensive deposit insurance scheme.The statistics presented are compelling: over 98 percent of depositors representing more than 281 million accounts across insured institutions are fully covered in the event of an insured financial institution's failure. This extensive coverage demonstrates the NDIC's commitment to safeguarding the interests of depositors nationwide.The address highlighted the importance of balancing traditional banking stability with emerging fintech innovations. As Nigeria's financial sector undergoes recapitalization efforts, the NDIC's role becomes increasingly vital in maintaining depositor confidence and system stability.The retreat provided a platform for dialogue between regulatory bodies and legislative oversight committees, ensuring coordinated efforts in strengthening Nigeria's financial architecture. By emphasizing the protective mechanisms in place, Mr. Sunday reinforced public trust in the banking system during a period of significant sector transformation.The NDIC's expanded insurance coverage and proactive engagement with stakeholders demonstrate its commitment to fostering a secure and resilient financial environment for all Nigerians.
