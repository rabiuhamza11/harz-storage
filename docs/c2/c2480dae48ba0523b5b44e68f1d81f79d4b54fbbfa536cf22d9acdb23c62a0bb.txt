What is being paid currently cannot take us anywhere," he said."We are also requesting that the backlog of gratuities between 2020 and 2025 be cleared. We believe this is doable before 2027," Obadimu added.The congress, themed "Healthy Life in Retirement," featured a health talk titled "Ageing Realities with Special Emphasis on Hypertension and Diabetes."Obadimu said the theme was chosen to draw attention to the health and well-being of retired educators. He urged members to undergo regular medical check-ups and adhere to medical advice to reduce the risks associated with hypertension and diabetes.On the 2027 general elections, the ARPSSON president urged members to participate actively and shun violence."We have informed our members to go and cast their votes.
