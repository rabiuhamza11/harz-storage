David HuttJournalist covering Europe-Southeast Asia relations @davidhuttjournoDavid has reported extensively on Southeast Asian politics and foreign relations for over a decade. David has covered Europe-Southeast Asian relations for DW since 2020. He began his journalistic career in Latin America before moving to Cambodia, working first for the Southeast Asia Globe and then Asia Times. David is a Southeast Asia columnist at the Diplomat and writes commentary for Radio Free Asia, Nikkei Asia and other international publications.
