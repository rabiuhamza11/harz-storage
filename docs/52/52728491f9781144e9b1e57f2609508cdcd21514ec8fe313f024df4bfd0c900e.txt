NDIC Celebrates Women's Empowerment at International Women's Day 2026 Observance The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) hosted a memorable International Women's Day (IWD) 2026 celebration, bringing together senior management and the female workforce to commemorate the occasion and celebrate women's contributions to institutional excellence. N NDIC April 2, 2026 1 min read The Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) hosted a memorable International Women's Day (IWD) 2026 celebration, bringing together senior management and the female workforce to commemorate the occasion and celebrate women's contributions to institutional excellence.Senior management presence was notably strong, with Managing Director/Chief Executive Mr. Thompson Oludare Sunday, Executive Director Corporate Services Mrs. Emily Osuji, and Executive Director Operations Dr. Kabir Katata in full attendance. Their presence underscored organizational commitment to gender inclusion and women's empowerment initiatives.The event featured distinguished speakers and panelists who educated and motivated the female workforce. The presentations focused on empowering women to embrace the Corporation's core values, recognize opportunities for continuous professional growth, and take active roles in advancing institutional objectives.The IWD 2026 celebration created a platform for recognizing the invaluable contributions of women across all levels of the NDIC. The event highlighted the importance of diversity and inclusion in achieving organizational excellence and delivering on the Corporation's mandate to protect depositors and maintain financial system stability.By commemorating International Women's Day, the NDIC demonstrated commitment to fostering an inclusive workplace where women are encouraged to develop their potential, assume leadership positions, and contribute meaningfully to institutional success.The celebration generated great memories and reinforced organizational values centered on respect, equality, and inclusive growth. Such initiatives are essential for building a high-performing, motivated workforce capable of delivering excellent service to depositors and stakeholders nationwide.
