[Business](https://dailypost.ng/business/)Dollar-to-Naira exchange rate Today, October 16, 2026: Naira depreciates against USD to end week on negative notePublished on October 16, 2026By [Ogaga Ariemu](https://dailypost.ng/author/ogaga-ariemu/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/16/dollar-to-naira-exchange-rate-today-october-16-2026-naira-depreciates-against-usd-to-end-week-on-negative-note/comments/) The naira depreciated against the United States dollar at the official foreign exchange market on Friday. Central Bank of Nigeria, CBN, data showed that the naira weakened to ₦1,332.26 per dollar on Friday, from ₦1,331.61 recorded on Thursday. This means that, on a day-to-day basis, the naira depreciated by ₦0.65 against the dollar.
