Key takeaways Migraine can be triggered by a combination of genetic and environmental factors, with people assigned female at birth and those with a family history of migraine being more susceptible. Lifestyle factors such as irregular sleep patterns, dehydration, skipping meals, and high stress levels can all increase the likelihood of experiencing a migraine. External stimuli, including strong odors, bright lights, weather changes, and even intense physical activity, can also act as migraine triggers for some individuals. A combination of genetics and environmental factors may contribute to migraine. You may be more likely to experience migraine if you have a family history of the condition. People assigned female at birth are also more likely to experience migraine, likely as a result of the hormone changes associated with menstruation, pregnancy, and menopause.
