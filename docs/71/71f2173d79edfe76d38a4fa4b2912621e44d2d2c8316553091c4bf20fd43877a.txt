[Business](https://dailypost.ng/business/)Food Inflation: Three Nigerian states with slowest price hikes in SeptemberPublished on October 16, 2026By [Ogaga Ariemu](https://dailypost.ng/author/ogaga-ariemu/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/16/food-inflation-three-nigerian-states-with-slowest-price-hikes-in-september/comments/) Adamawa, Kano and Bauchi recorded the slowest month-on-month increases in food inflation among Nigerian states in September 2026, amid rising food price pressures across the country. This is according to the National Bureau of Statistics, NBS, September 2026 Consumer Price Index and Inflation Report. The data showed that food inflation rose at the slowest pace in Adamawa(-13.84 percent), Kano(-11.01 per cent) and Bauchi(-10.21) on a month-on-month basis, indicating relatively slower increases in food prices in the three states during the period under review.
