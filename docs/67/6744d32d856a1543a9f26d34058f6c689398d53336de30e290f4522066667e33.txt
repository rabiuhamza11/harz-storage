Abubakar Abdullahi, 28; Babangida Ya’u, 35; Babannan Hassan, 27; and Isah Isibu, 35.The police spokesperson further disclosed that on October 15, 2026, operatives of the Maigatari Police Division, working with the Border Patrol Team, arrested another suspect, Isa Yusuf, 35, from Hantsu village in Miga Local Government Area.He said the suspect was arrested during a routine patrol at Maigatari Market, where officers recovered 13 new iTel mobile phones, 13 chargers, 13 batteries and N102,500 in cash.According to Shiisu, the suspect confessed during interrogation at the State Criminal Investigation Department (SCID) in Dutse to being a member of the gang responsible for the Jahun robbery incidents.He added that investigations confirmed that the recovered items were part of the property stolen during the attacks.Shiisu said the six suspects had been charged to court, while efforts were ongoing to arrest other fleeing members of the gang and recover the remaining stolen property.Share this story[WhatsApp](https://wa.me/?text=Police%20arrest%20six%20over%20alleged%20armed%20robbery%20in%20Jigawa%20%E2%80%8E%20%E2%80%8E%20https%3A%2F%2Fdailypost.ng%2F2026%2F10%2F20%2Fpolice-arrest-six-suspects-over-armed-robbery-in-jigawa%2F) FacebookYou may like[Lagos shuts Ejigbo Fire Station over repeated attacks on firefighters](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/19/lagos-shuts-ejigbo-fire-station-over-repeated-attacks-on-firefighters-2/)[Two severely burnt as diesel tanker plunges from Otedola bridge in Lagos](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/19/two-severely-burnt-as-diesel-tanker-plunges-from-otedola-bridge-in-lagos/)[Tanker catches fire under Otedola Bridge, Lagos](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/19/tanker-catches-fire-under-otedola-bridge-lagos/)[Join the conversation](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/20/police-arrest-six-suspects-over-armed-robbery-in-jigawa/comments/)
