[Entertainment](https://dailypost.ng/entertainment/)‘I defeated 17 Governors, won’t support APC’ – DavidoPublished on October 16, 2026By [Vanessa Onah](https://dailypost.ng/author/vanessa-onah/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/16/i-defeated-17-governors-wont-support-apc-davido/comments/) Popular Nigerian singer David Adeleke, popularly known as Davido, has ruled out supporting the All Progressives Congress, APC, ahead of the 2027 presidential election, despite his uncle, Osun State Governor Ademola Adeleke, endorsing President Bola Tinubu. In a recent video making the rounds online, the Grammy-nominated artist made his position known during an interview with News Central TV, just days after the Osun State governorship election and the announcement of the results. The singer explained that although his uncle, Governor Ademola Adeleke, declared support for President Bola Tinubu after winning the election, he was not obligated to follow his uncle’s political choice.
