Details of the conference are as follows: Theme: DRIVING ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT THROUGH A SUSTAINABLE PENSION INDUSTRY Date: Tuesday 15 and Wednesday 16 October, 2019 Time: 8:30am Venue: Lagos-Osun Hall, Transcorp Hilton Hotel, Maitama, Abuja Attendance is open to all Licensed Pension Operators and each Operator is required to nominate four (4) Non-Executive Directors, of which at least one must be an Independent Director, to attend the Conference.
