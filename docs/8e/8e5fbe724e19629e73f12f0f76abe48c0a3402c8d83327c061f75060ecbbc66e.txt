Key takeaways COPD may involve symptoms like a persistent cough, shortness of breath, and recurring respiratory infections, which may worsen over time. Fatigue is a common symptom of COPD, often leading to severe exhaustion and affecting your ability to perform daily tasks. Advanced COPD may cause symptoms like headaches, swollen feet and ankles, and unintentional weight loss, requiring prompt medical attention. Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is a chronic condition that causes inflammation and damage to the lungs. Over time, COPD could make it difficult to breathe and impact your daily activities. At first, symptoms of COPD may be mild. But as lung damage progresses, symptoms may worsen. In some cases, these may become life threatening. Keep reading to learn more about the symptoms of COPD.
