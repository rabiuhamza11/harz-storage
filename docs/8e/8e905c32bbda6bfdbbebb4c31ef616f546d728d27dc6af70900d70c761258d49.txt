Gabriel](https://dailypost.ng/author/john-gabriel/)[Comment](https://dailypost.ng/2026/10/19/naf-neutralizes-terrorist-figures-in-precision-air-strike/comments/)The Nigerian Air Force (NAF), operating under the Joint Task Force, Operation HADIN KAI (OPHK), has recorded a significant operational success against terrorists in the Lake Chad region following a precision air strike on an identified terrorist hideout on October 19, 2026.This was contained in a statement by Air Commodore Ehimen Ejodame, Director of Public Relations and Information, Headquarters, Nigerian Air Force.According to the statement, the operation underscores the NAF’s sustained commitment to degrading terrorist networks, disrupting their logistics and enhancing security across Nigeria’s North-East."Preliminary post-strike assessments indicate that several prominent Islamic State West Africa Province (ISWAP) fighters were neutralised, including individuals identified as key figures within the group’s security structure and leadership council, like Jindullah, reportedly the Chief Security Guard and confidant of Abu Saleem as well as Abul Kissa, a member of ISWAP Shura Council," the statement
