In the age of influencers, algorithms and listicles, Chang cuts through the noise on a classic American road trip following local leads and friends’ recommendations to his next meal. Episodes Season 1 Nashville to Madisonville with Christina Tosi David Chang and longtime friend Christina Tosi, founder of Milk Bar, make a pilgrimage to the legendary Benton's smokehouse in Madisonville, Tennessee that helped shape their early careers. The two eat everything they see along the way. LA to Vegas with Cord Jefferson Many have made the drive from LA to Las Vegas in search of fortune, glory, and… other vices. David Chang and Oscar-winner Cord Jefferson are heading into the desert to show that Vegas might just be the best eating city in America. Dallas to San Antonio with Cristela Alonzo David Chang is driving through Texas to find out what's new with barbecue, while his companion, comedian and Texas native Cristela Alonzo, introduces him to her side of the Lone Star State and the cuisine she grew up eating. Sonoma to the Pacific with Chris Ying David Chang joins best friend and creative partner Chris Ying for a wine-soaked romp through Northern California, where Chris tries to convince Chang to love the region's cuisine as they debate whether Mother Nature is behind its quality. Trailer Chef David Chang puts down his phone and hits the open road with various friends in search of deliciousness - ignoring influencers, algorithms, and listicles - as they scour the country following local leads and their own instincts.
