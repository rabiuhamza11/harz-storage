CONDITIONS FOR BIDDING FOR THE ASSETS1)Bids should be submitted in a sealed envelope together with a deposit of 10% of the bid price in Certified Bank Draft in favour of the respective primary mortgage banks (In-Liquidation) and with an inscription “BID FOR ASSETS,” on the top left hand corner of the envelope. The bid should contain the name, address and telephone numbers of the bidder. The successful bidder will be required to pay the balance of the bid price within 24 hours of winning the bid.2) Financial Institutions will be given preference.3)The 10% draft for the unsuccessful bidders would be returned immediately.4)Bids should be submitted to the Auctioneers or NDIC Contact persons within the stated period.5)Submitted bids shall be opened before the bidders at the various locations.6)All Assets are sold as “as is” and “net of all taxes”.7)All purchased items must be evacuated within 24 hours of purchase.
