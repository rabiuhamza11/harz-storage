Key Takeaways Major depressive disorder (MDD) is a common mood disorder characterized by persistent sadness and a loss of interest in activities, impacting mood, behavior, and physical functions. Symptoms of MDD can vary but often also include irritability, fatigue, changes in sleep or appetite, and thoughts of suicide. These symptoms can manifest differently in teens and older adults. Treatment for MDD typically involves a combination of medication and psychotherapy, alongside lifestyle adjustments like dietary changes, exercise, and maintaining a consistent sleep schedule.
