Episodes More Like This Season 1 Season 2 Season 3 Season 4 Season 5 Season 6 Season 7 Season 8 Season 9 Season 10 Season 11 Season 12 Season 1 Myanmar Anthony Bourdain explores one of the most fabled and beautiful areas of Asia: Myanmar, the city of Yangon and the remote Bagan temple. Koreatown Anthony Bourdain visits Koreatown in Los Angeles, where he finds a tight-knit community marked by the 1992 Rodney King riots. Colombia Colombia has changed immensely over the past 10 years. Anthony Bourdain explores several regions of the country, from the mountains down to the Caribbean coast. Quebec Chefs and restauranteurs Fred Morin and Dave McMillan, of Joe Beef's, take us across Canada by rail. Tangier (Morocco) Anthony Bourdain explores the "Interzone," where artists like Burroughs, Bowles and the Rolling Stones sought escape from Western moral prohibitions. Libya Anthony Bourdain explores Libyan hip-hop, Italian restaurants and post-war uncertainty, by examining the country through personal stories, food and music. Peru Come with Anthony Bourdain and world-renowned chef Eric Ripert as they travel back in time from modern Lima into pre-Columbian Peru. Congo Anthony Bourdain visits Congo, the setting of one of his favorite books, Conrad's "Heart of Darkness." Season 2 Jerusalem Anthony Bourdain's first trip to Israel, the West Bank and Gaza is focused on visiting with local chefs, home cooks, writers and amateur foodies. Spain Anthony Bourdain visits Andalucia during Semana Santa, Holy Week, to visit longtime director Zach Zamboni, who currently lives in Granada. New Mexico With a combination of cultures, New Mexico has the largest Hispanic population per capita in the US. Go with Anthony Bourdain as he explores. Copenhagen Home to chef Rene Redzepi of Noma, Copenhagen is known as the cultural center of Denmark. Anthony Bourdain explores its fantastic food and natural beauty. Sicily Anthony Bourdain visits Sicily, where he goes fishing for his supper and sets out to find a home-cooked meal. South Africa Anthony Bourdain visits the Johannesburg Suburb of Hillbrow, where he spends a day with a taxi driver while absorbing the culture and of course, food. Tokyo Go with Anthony Bourdain as he travels to Tokyo to experience new aspects of their culture and cuisine, and the dark underside of the city. Detroit Take a look at Detroit's history with Anthony Bourdain — from the dramatic economic rise and fall to the promising future it holds. Season 3 Punjab Anthony Bourdain soaks up all Amritsar has to offer. Go with him as he samples savory delicacies at roadside restaurants and experiences a Gurpurab festival. Las Vegas Anthony Bourdain travels to Las Vegas with food author Michael Ruhlman, where they explore sites including Huntridge Tavern. Lyon Anthony Bourdain accompanies world-renowned chef Daniel Boulud as they travel back to his hometown of Lyon, France. Mexico Anthony Bourdain travels to Mexico City, Oaxaca and Cuernavaca, Mexico, where residents express their passion through food and art. Russia On the eve of the Olympic games in Sochi, Anthony Bourdain visits Russia. He confers with locals and takes the Grand Express train to St. Petersburg. Mississippi Anthony Bourdain takes in Mississippi history, including downtown Jackson's Big Apply Inn, known for its Pig's Ear Sandwich and role in the Civil Rights era. Thailand Anthony Bourdain takes to the streets of Thailand, where he meets a local celebrity while on the hunt for some late-night grub. Brazil Go with Anthony Bourdain as he visits Bahia, the "African heart of Brazil" recognized for its Afro-Brazilian music, art, design and food. Season 4 Shanghai Anthony Bourdain explores Shanghai by savoring home-style cuisine and having meaningful conversations with locals about the culture and the booming economy. The Bronx Anthony Bourdain explores the diverse cuisines and cultures The Bronx, New York, has to offer, by experiencing firsthand the music, food and art. Paraguay Dive into Anthony Bourdain's puzzling family history, as he takes a trip to Paraguay and experiences the rich culture and savory local dishes. Vietnam Travel with Anthony Bourdain as he gives an inside look at the Dong Ba market, Vinh Moc tunnels, Duyen Anh's restaurant and the Citadel. Tanzania Anthony Bourdain visits Tanzania, where he enjoys the island's famous food and comes face to face with lions and elephants while on an excursion. Iran Anthony Bourdain explores Tehran and Isfahan, where he soaks up the beauty of the country and the complexity of its foods. Massachusetts Bourdain takes a trip to the haunts of his youth that marked the start of his culinary career, and he candidly discusses his descent into heroin addiction and recovery. He then heads to the western part of the state. Jamaica Anthony Bourdain travels to Jamaica, where he visits the home of James Bond author Ian Fleming and learns about music legend Bob Marley. Season 5 Korea Anthony Bourdain returns to Korea after 10 years to experience hyper-modern Seoul, and taste Korean fried chicken, vintage army stew and Kimchi. Miami Anthony Bourdain gets a taste of Miami with cow foot soup in Little Haiti and a personal lesson on sights and sounds from musician Questlove. Scotland Anthony Bourdain visits one of his favorite cities in Europe — Glasgow— where he makes his first stop at the Old College Bar for a pint. Budapest Bourdain explores the beauty, culture, and food of Budapest, where he samples goulash, fisherman's soup, blood sausage, and chicken liver. Madagascar Anthony Bourdain explores the island nation of Madagascar, where he visits the capital city of Tana and samples Malagasy cuisine. New Jersey Anthony Bourdain travels to his childhood home state with his brother Chris, where they rekindle memories at the classic roadside joint, Hiram's. Hawaii There's another side to Hawaii that many tourists don't know about. Go with Anthony Bourdain as he explores the unknown area. Beirut Anthony Bourdain travels to Beirut, where he discovers a beautiful country known for its vibrant nightlife and legendary cuisine. Season 6 Cuba Anthony Bourdain explores Cuba: from its bustling capital city Havana, to the music mecca of Santiago. Marseille Anthony Bourdain and chef Eric Ripert visit France's oldest city to experience its stew of cultures and cuisines. Okinawa Anthony Bourdain explores the enchanting island of Okinawa, taking in the historical, political, cultural and culinary perspectives. Bay Area Anthony Bourdain ventures to the Bay Area, where he tries Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, discovers healing through nourishment and enjoys soul food at Real Miss Ollie's. Ethiopia Anthony Bourdain visits Addis Ababa, to sample dishes like injera bread and soak up Ethiopian melodies with chef Marcus Samuelsson. Borneo Anthony Bourdain returns to Borneo after 10 years to enjoy chicken rendang and pork noodles in the street food paradise of Kuala Lumpur. Istanbul Anthony Bourdain explores the mixing of food, culture and politics in Istanbul, as he enjoys an authentic Turkish breakfast and watches an oil wrestling match. Charleston Anthony Bourdain and Charleston resident Bill Murray dine on traditional Gullah cuisine, oyster pie, shrimp and grits. Season 7 Manila: The Philippines Anthony Bourdain explores the Philippines at Christmas and eats at beloved chicken chain Jollibee, samples sisig and indulges in the popular dessert halo-halo. Chicago Anthony Bourdain visits the iconic Old Town Ale House, eats mapo tofu in Chinatown and shares a home-cooked meal with rapper Lupe Fiasco. The Greek Islands On the island of Naxos, Anthony Bourdain dives to a sunken shipwreck, drinks raki with locals and samples cuisine fresh off a fishing boat. Montana Anthony Bourdain visits the Crow Reservation, hunts with podcast host Joe Rogan and eats dinner at Butte institution Lydia's Supper Club. Tbilisi, Georgia Zamir Gotta upstages Anthony Bourdain on a tour of the country situated in the Caucasus, featuring food from Tbilisi, Batumi and Khurvaleti. Senegal Anthony Bourdain explores the West African nation with chef Pierre Thiam, journalist Ofeibea Quist-Arcton and famed musician Youssou N'Dour. Cologne, Germany The famed Carnival celebration sets the tone for Anthony Bourdain's visit to this city that boasts Kolsch, the raw minced pork dish mett and blood sausage. Buenos Aires Anthony Bourdain's trip to this city renowned for its love of meat features a meal at the famed Don Carlos and an after-hours soccer match. Season 8 Hanoi During a meal of the Vietnamese capital's signature dish, bun cha, President Barack Obama shares personal stories and his travel history with Anthony Bourdain. Nashville In Music City, Anthony Bourdain dines at the Catbird Seat, samples Bolton's famous hot chicken and has a night out with singer Alison Mosshart and her band. Sichuan with Eric Ripert Anthony Bourdain introduces his friend Eric Ripert to China's Sichuan province, sampling the region's famously spicy dishes, including green peppercorn fish. London After the Brexit vote, Anthony Bourdain dines on roast bone marrow at St. John, eats Scotch eggs with Nigella Lawson and meets artist Ralph Steadman. Houston In this diverse city, Anthony Bourdain takes in a quinceanera, learns about slab culture and dines on Viet-Cajun crawfish, barbecue and Congolese-Cajun stew. Japan with Masa Guided by chef Masa Takayama, Anthony Bourdain returns to Japan and tours food markets, speaks with a former geisha and dines at the acclaimed Ginza Sushiko. Minas Gerais, Brazil Anthony Bourdain explores the scenic Brazilian region known for its baroque architecture and samples mineira cuisine during a traditional country meal. Season 9 Los Angeles On this tour of Los Angeles, Anthony Bourdain focuses on the impact and contributions of the city's often overlooked Latino communities. San Sebastian Anthony Bourdain visits the coastal resort city in Spain's Basque Country famous for its spectacular views and its seafood-centric cuisine. Laos The country's rich food culture is on display as Anthony Bourdain also examines the impact of the Secret War waged by the US during the Laotian Civil War. Queens Back in his hometown, Anthony Bourdain discovers a bustling hub of cuisines and cultures from around the world in this borough of New York City. Antarctica At the bottom of the world, Anthony Bourdain experiences the beauty and mystery of the most extreme, isolated place on Earth. Oman Anthony Bourdain takes his first journey to this sultanate on the Arabian Peninsula and finds a beautiful land that defies expectations. Trinidad During a trip to the Caribbean, Anthony Bourdain explores the island's history of colonialism and slavery and a food culture reflecting its diverse population. Porto, Portugal Food takes center stage in this tour of the Portuguese coastal city, where Anthony Bourdain's travel companions include his former boss, chef Jose Meirelles. Season 10 Singapore Touring the Southeast Asian country, Anthony Bourdain immerses himself in the array of multicultural cuisines found throughout this island city-state. French Alps Anthony Bourdain and his longtime friend, renowned chef Eric Ripert, embark on a ski-and-cheese-filled journey through the French Alps. Lagos People are the focus of Anthony Bourdain's visit to this bustling metropolis, where he explores the region's food, music and rich cultural diversity. Pittsburgh Arriving in a Steel City at a crossroads, Anthony Bourdain delves into comfort food classics and the new restaurants reflecting the burgeoning tech industry. Sri Lanka Following the end of the country's long civil war, Anthony Bourdain travels to the once-divided island nation to experience its people and their food cultures. Puerto Rico Amid the backdrop of Puerto Rico's economic crisis, Anthony Bourdain goes beyond the resorts to explore its people, the island's natural beauty and its food. Seattle Anthony Bourdain immerses himself in the sights, sounds and tastes of this Pacific Northwest city. Season 11 West Virginia Exploring this often-misunderstood state, Anthony Bourdain traverses a mine, observes the sport of rock bouncing and dines on signature Appalachian dishes. Uruguay Returning after a decade, Anthony Bourdain finds a progressive nation undergoing change, though colonial remnants are still evident, most notably in its food. Newfoundland Anthony Bourdain explores this island off Canada's eastern coast, a place abundant in fish and wild game and steeped in distinctive traditions. Armenia Bourdain takes a wide-angle look at the culture and history of this country, heavily influenced by Europe and Russia, with guides musician of Armenian descent Serj Tankian, and historian/Armenian resident Richard Giragosian. Cajun Mardi Gras Celebrating Louisiana's Cajun culture, Anthony Bourdain takes part in the region's less familiar Mardi Gras traditions, which can be traced to medieval France. Bhutan Joined by director Darren Aronofsky, Anthony Bourdain travels through the remote kingdom on the Lateral Road, famed for its spectacular views of the Himalayas. Berlin In the vibrant German capital, Anthony Bourdain encounters an open and accepting society teeming with unbridled creativity at odds with the city's grim history. Season 12 Kenya Anthony Bourdain introduces "United Shades of America" host W. Kamau Bell to the East African nation's distinctive sights, sounds and tastes. Asturias, Spain Visiting the northern Spanish coastal region of Asturias, Anthony Bourdain joins chef and humanitarian Jose Andres as he returns to his homeland. Indonesia A shadow play provides insight into this country comprised of thousands of islands and a tumultuous history, a place in which food often acts as a unifier. Bourdain's Impact Anthony Bourdain's unique storytelling ability greatly impacted how people engaged with food and travel, influencing how we interact with the world around us. Far West Texas Anthony Bourdain travels to the rugged Big Bend region of Texas, an area near the Mexican border that often pits man against nature. Lower East Side On a personal journey through this formerly bohemian Manhattan neighborhood, Anthony Bourdain reflects with cultural trailblazers as they dine. Behind the Scenes Anthony Bourdain's relationship with his crew, the close-knit team that traveled around the world with him, was unlike any other in his life. Anthony Bourdain: Parts Unknown Prime Cuts Anthony Bourdain revisits unforgettable moments from Parts Unknown with curated highlights, rare behind-the-scenes outtakes, and his signature, thought-provoking commentary.
